# Design decisions

Status values: Proposed (a roast implies the rule, only the owner or the main session can accept it), Accepted (a standing rule in `CLAUDE.md` or set by the owner), Implemented (shipped), Reconsidered (new evidence reopened it). DES-001 to DES-012 are the accepted standing rules. DES-013 on are proposals from the first roasts.

| Id | Decision | Reasoning | Alternatives | Applies to | Date | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DES-001 | Show it, do not say it. Encode meaning in form: an icon, a state, a position, a colour, a weight or the control itself. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Every screen | 2026-11-03 | Accepted |
| DES-002 | Let the thing be the control. If a control needs a label to explain it, the mapping is weak. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Every screen | 2026-11-03 | Accepted |
| DES-003 | Build hierarchy, not volume. The most important thing is the most obvious and secondary detail recedes. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Every screen | 2026-11-03 | Accepted |
| DES-004 | Disclose progressively. The common path first, detail one level deeper. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Every screen | 2026-11-03 | Accepted |
| DES-005 | Restraint, not emptiness. Never hide what the traveller needs, give it a better form. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Every screen | 2026-11-03 | Accepted |
| DES-006 | Text growing inside a component is a design problem. Rethink the component with apple-design, emil-design-eng and 21st.dev instead of appending a line. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Every component | 2026-11-03 | Accepted |
| DES-007 | An icon that carries meaning gets an accessible name and a tooltip, and lives in the app's own icon set. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Planner and web map | 2026-11-03 | Accepted |
| DES-008 | No question marks in the UI. Use words or omit. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Every screen | 2026-11-03 | Accepted (owner feedback) |
| DES-009 | No summary prose lines, such as a recap sentence of dates, nights and places. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Every screen | 2026-11-03 | Accepted (owner feedback) |
| DES-010 | Plain sentences. No semicolons as separators and no em or en dashes anywhere, in docs, commit messages or UI text. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Docs, commits, UI | 2026-11-03 | Accepted |
| DES-011 | Prices are Danish kroner and times are local clock times. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. | None recorded. | Every screen | 2026-11-03 | Accepted |
| DES-012 | Every finished page gets a roast from a fresh reviewer against its journey, and the findings go to this log before any fix. | Standing rule in CLAUDE.md or owner feedback. Reopened 2026-11-04 by the workflow refactor: a roast is now something Simon asks for when he wants an honest look at a screen, not a gate on every page, and only what he accepts from it reaches this log. The fresh reviewer part survives in the `ui-roast` skill. | A roast as a mandatory stage after every build (the original rule, dropped because it cost a two agent pipeline per page and recorded every observation). | Process | 2026-11-03 | Reconsidered |
| DES-013 | An unknown value is never rendered as zero and never as the words "unknown" or "not known". It is a count with a hollow mark, a hatched segment or a dimmed chip. Totals that contain unknowns use an outline style and complete totals use a solid one. | Drafts looked cheapest only because unpriced legs counted as nothing, and the phrase "not known" is text standing in for a state. | Keep zeros but add a footnote (rejected, it is prose). | trips-list, trip-page, flight-sheets, map-tab, web-map | 2026-11-03 | Implemented |
| DES-014 | One booked or open marker on every leg of every mode. Done goes quiet (muted with a small check), open gets the colour and an inline action such as Pick or Book. | Open items must be the most obvious thing, and today they differ from done by a one pixel border. Ground legs have no state at all. | Keep the green outlined chip and only add an open chip (partial, ground legs still unmarked). | trip-page, trip-map, trips-list, flight-sheets | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-015 | One colour, one job. Hue is spent only on the three modes (flight gold `--flight`, ground teal `--bus`, boat blue `--boat`), booked (green `--booked`), warning (orange `--warn`, always with its triangle), error (red `--err`) and the score (a violet ramp `--score-top`, `--score-mid`, `--score-low`). Everything else is carried by form. Selected is a neutral lift, a 2 px ring in `--sel` on a raised surface, and the rest dims per DES-029. Saved is a filled glyph. Potential is desaturated `--reach`. The primary action is the one light filled button in a view (`--accent` on `--accent-ink`), and focus uses it too. Cheaper and best are marker shapes, not colours. Open legs keep their mode colour with an inline action, booked legs go quiet with a green check, per DES-014. On the map, pattern means mode (flight dotted, ground solid, boat dashed) and saturation with weight means in the trip. Fly in and fly home are positions, marked by a ring with a landing or take off glyph, not by a hue. Both apps share one token set with the same names and values. One mode vocabulary is used on map, bar, timeline and chips, and the phone never drops a glyph. | Gold, yellow and amber meant flight, selected, planned, primary, picked and best. Review-012 counted nine jobs on `--flight` in the planner CSS and six on the two greens, and found that every non flight job reads the token directly, so the fix is new job tokens and a selector pass, not new values. | Per screen colour rules (rejected, they erode the mode encoding). A reserved action hue instead of the light fill (open, taste). A neutral lightness ramp for the score (rejected, a low score would read as dimmed or potential on the map). Keep green for fly in and give booked another hue (rejected, booked green is learned and kept in review-006 and review-011). | All screens | 2026-11-04, first written 2026-11-03 | Proposed, rewritten by review-012 |
| DES-016 | Every panel sets a camera that fits its subject, with padding equal to the panel width on desktop and the sheet height on the phone. Nothing ever zooms out to a world view. | Panels now cover the thing they describe and the route shrinks to a cluster. | A fixed padding for all panels (breaks on the phone). | trip-map, map-tab, web-map, city | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-017 | One phone sheet standard: a grabber, three detents (peek, middle, full), one close, scrolling only at full. One compact map control column inside the safe area that moves with the sheet. No zoom buttons on touch. A legend lives behind one layers button. | Four screens show the controls sliding off the left edge or covering the route, and sheets differ between screens. | Hide controls whenever a sheet is open (loses the layer toggles). | city, map-tab, web-map, trip-map, trips-list | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-018 | On the phone nothing scrolls inside a scrolling page. Matrices, grids and wide tables become ranked cards with the price large. | Price columns fall off screen and the nested scroll boxes fight the page. | Responsive tables with pinned columns (still two scrolling axes). | way-picker, trips-list, web-map, flight-sheets | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-019 | The primary action is the one for the current journey step. Save trip is quiet or autosaves, New trip is secondary, a blind commit is never the loudest button, and search comes before commit when a price is missing. | Save trip, New trip and the leg commit button outrank the trip itself. | Keep Save trip loud but add a dirty state (still loud when nothing changed). | trip-page, trips-list, map-tab, flight-sheets, city | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-020 | One component per object. The city is the same header, section order (stay, do, train) and counts on the trip sheet and the map panel. One date picker, one look. Frames differ and content does not. | The same city has two designs and the date has two controls. | Keep both and align the headers only. | city, flight-sheets, web-map | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-021 | One nights scaled timeline component, with segments butted, widths proportional to nights, a tick for a zero night pass and a distinct today needle. It is used by the trip map bar, the trips list rows and the compare tray, all on the same axis. | Length and overlap between options cannot be read from equal width beads or chips. | A numeric nights column only (does not show where options differ). | trip-map, trips-list, trip-page | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-022 | No build metadata or cryptic chips in the top bar. A freshness warning is an icon with a clear unit and shows only when something needs attention. | The build line sits in the most valuable status corner on four screens. | Move it to a footer (still noise). | trip-page, trips-list, way-picker, map-tab | 2026-11-03 | Implemented |
| DES-023 | A booked thing is locked. Its date chip carries a lock glyph and does not open a picker, and alternatives sit behind Change booking. | Booked legs open price grids and cheaper day chips that invite a tap. | Warn on tap (a sentence after the fact). | flight-sheets, trip-page | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-024 | Moving a date shows its effect before it commits: later stops shifting, the night change, the cost delta, and a lock for any day that clashes with a booked leg. | The main action moves the whole trip blind. | Undo after the move (the cost lands anyway). | flight-sheets, trip-page | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-025 | One format for money and one for durations, always with the unit. Hours and minutes everywhere, never decimal hours. This is the Danish kroner formatter applied in one shared function. | Money is written four ways and hours two ways in the same flow. | Per screen formatting (what exists). | way-picker, flight-sheets, map-tab, web-map, trip-page | 2026-11-03 | Implemented |
| DES-026 | Raw data never becomes user text. Structured booking fields are separate from notes written by the traveller. Carrier lists are normalised and deduplicated. Credits sit behind an info icon. | Notes show pasted database strings and carrier lists show mixed case duplicates. | Strip text on save only (the stored mess remains). | flight-sheets, web-map, city, trips-list | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-027 | A trip page leads with a status row: total nights with dates, cost with a paid share, and booked and open counts as chips that jump to the first open leg. | The page never says how long the trip is or what is still open. | A status line of text (rejected, prose). | trip-page, trips-list | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-028 | The signed out web map opens on the research: no sign in row, no empty planner, Options renamed for what it holds and made primary, and gyms and experiences removed from the guest view. | A guest meets an account form and an empty state before the map. | Keep the sign in row but make it smaller. | web-map | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-029 | Selection draws and dims in three tiers. The selected thing is the hero with its legs lit, the past is quiet, the future is plain. The camera follows. | Selecting a country, an option or a connection changes almost nothing on the map. | Two tiers (the chosen country then looks like a dead end). | trip-map, map-tab, web-map | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-030 | Map layers sit on the trip instead of replacing it. The route stays drawn and dimmed, the trip card stays, layers toggle independently and the default scope is along the route. | Gyms and experiences hide the route and rank the whole continent. | Keep layers as exclusive tabs. | map-tab | 2026-11-03 | Proposed |
| DES-031 | A cost shared by several legs is shown once as the ticket's, with chips for the legs it covers and the current leg marked, never as a fact of a single leg. Any total that sums legs counts a shared ticket once. | Review-011 found the paid amount and the same ticket reference repeated on every leg of a shared booking, so a trip total summing legs would double count it. | Repeat the full amount on each leg (the current shape, invites double counting). | flight-sheets, trip-page, trips-list | 2026-11-04 | Proposed |
