How ratings work

All ratings are out of 10. Green is 8 or more, amber 5 to 7.9, red below 5.

Whole trips

  • Rating = 2 × (0.35 cost + 0.35 hours + 0.15 way home + 0.15 hassle). Cost and travel hours weigh the same.
  • Cost score: 5 at 7,000 kr or less, minus 1 per 2,000 kr more (minimum 1). Cost is the total: flights, cabin bag fees, hub stays, and 300 kr for a bed whenever a regional flight lands between 00:00 and 05:59 (the room has to be booked from the night before).
  • Hours score: 5 at 34 travel hours or less, minus 1 per 8 hours more (minimum 1). Travel hours add the way in, the way home and every regional flight with its layovers, plus 2 hours for the Boliviana hops inside Bolivia. One travel hour weighs about as much as 250 kr.
  • Way home score: 5, minus 1 per hub night, minus 1 for a change between separate tickets, minus 1 for US transit, minus 1 over 20 flying hours and 1 more over 23.
  • Hassle: the average of the way in (scored like the way home) and the regional flights (5, minus 1 per regional flight beyond two, minus 1 per flight leaving or landing between 00:00 and 05:00).
  • Scope: the trip starts in Central America (not Guatemala), visits Colombia and Bolivia at most once each, and ends in Central America or Bolivia.

Long-haul tickets

  • Rating = 2 × (0.5 price + 0.3 way home + 0.2 way in). Price score is 5 at 7,000 kr, minus 1 per 1,000 kr more (minimum 1), on the total including hub stays.
  • Ways are scored as above.
