# Simon's travel preferences

## What he wants from the trip
- Local life first: meeting locals, speaking Spanish, spontaneous encounters, places with their own feel. Being stuck among tourists only is what he fears most.
- Dancing and nightlife: salsa and Latin music, going out with locals and with backpackers.
- Backpacker company: social hostels where he meets other travellers, without it being a party-only scene.
- Island and beach vibes, and diving (Open Water certified, about 6 dives. Interested in sharks and bigger animals. Advanced only where it unlocks something worth it).
- Nature: welcome, and loves animals. Going on tourist excursions to for example volcanos and long hikes are welcome, but not highly ranked. A few highlights, not every stop. Same goes for Mayan ruins etc.
- Variety: each stop should add something different (city, island, mountains, culture, coast). Avoid back-to-back stops that feel the same if possible unless very close by (e.g. The Honduras Islands).


## How he travels
- Slow: about 1 to 2 weeks in places he likes, fewer and longer stops. Keep it simple and not over-packed. He wants freedom to decide on the ground.
- He does not want a very FIXED amount of days each place he wants flexibility on when to move on, so if he liked one place he can be for as much as he wants, or as little as he wants. Thats why dates works as just a general idea, but should be kept flexible.
- Overland inside a region by bus to see the country. Flights to jump between regions.
- Christmas and New Year somewhere social and festive with locals and travellers, christmas and new years can be seperate places, but be weary of flight prices and consider that, otherwise busses can also do. If both can be both places thats also welcome.
- Budget: hostels, street food, cheap living, with room for diving and experiences. About 650 to 700 kr a day all in.
- Bags: carry-on plus a cabin bag.

## Fixed parts of this trip
- About 2 Dec 2026 to 3 Feb 2027. Long haul is one combined ticket from and back to Copenhagen.
- Central America north to south first (likely starting in Guatemala City), Colombia with Medellín (Cali maybe, Bogotá only for transit), then Bolivia last: old friends in Cochabamba (he lived there years ago), La Paz and Santa Cruz. The last week in Cochabamba. Home from Santa Cruz.
- Mexico (Cancún, Playa del Carmen, Guadalajara) is an option, not a must.

## Flight rules
- At most 30 h in one direction. About 22 to 23 h preferred. Judge time and price together. A few hundred kroner don't matter.
- Avoid landings between 00:00 and 05:59, above all on the way home and at connections where he would need a bed.
- Momondo is the main search engine (hidden connections, multi-city). Never book or pay anything.
